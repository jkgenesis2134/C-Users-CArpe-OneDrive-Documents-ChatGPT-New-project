# CareerMaker user testing checklist

## Intake

- Upload TXT, Markdown, RTF, PDF, and DOCX samples.
- Confirm the filename and extraction status are visible.
- Confirm users can evaluate an upload without pasting.
- Try drag-and-drop and keyboard activation.
- Try an unsupported extension and a file above 20 MB.

## Evaluation

- Run Improve and confirm the score, paper type, verdict, dimensions, and revision priorities appear.
- Open Further evaluation.
- Open the peer review simulator.
- Check AI-writing limitations and research-integrity language.
- Run Humanize, Formalize, Simplify, Summarize, and Grammar.
- Download and copy a report.

## Research workflow

- Open the idea evaluator and submit an idea with and without a mechanism.
- Open journal match, conference opportunities, citation audit, and integrity review.
- Open submission formatter and verify manuscript, target, URL, and instructions fields.
- Open the research map preview.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior

- Navigate the primary controls with the keyboard.
- Confirm visible focus states.
- Test at a narrow mobile width.
- Toggle dark mode.
- Confirm no important warning relies on color alone.

## Known prototype boundaries

- No production AI provider is connected.
- No cloud storage, account, billing, or memory is active.
- Journal and conference data are not live or authoritative.
- PDF/DOCX extraction depends on browser-side libraries loading successfully.

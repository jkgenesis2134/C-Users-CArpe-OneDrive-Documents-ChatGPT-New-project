Privacy preview

Your unpublished research remains private.

This research preview does not require an account and does not intentionally save manuscript text, profiles, or document history after the session. Files and text are processed in the browser where supported.

What this version does

It accepts files locally, extracts readable text when browser tools are available, and provides prototype evaluation guidance. No production AI provider is connected in this version.

What future versions must explain

Before server-side AI, storage, or collaboration is enabled, CareerMaker must clearly disclose what is transmitted, which providers receive it, how long it is retained, how deletion works, and whether provider data is used for training.

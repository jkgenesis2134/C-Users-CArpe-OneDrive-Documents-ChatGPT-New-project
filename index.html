REVIEWER 1

Theory reviewer

Tests whether the contribution is theoretically distinct, the mechanism is clear, and the hypotheses follow from the literature.

Primary question: what does this explain that existing theory does not?
REVIEWER 2

Methods reviewer

Challenges design fit, sampling, measurement, analysis, causal language, robustness, and the limits of reported evidence.

Primary question: can this design support the conclusion?
REVIEWER 3

Journal reviewer

Assesses outlet fit, expected contribution, manuscript readiness, and the strongest likely objection at review.

Primary question: why this journal, and why now?
